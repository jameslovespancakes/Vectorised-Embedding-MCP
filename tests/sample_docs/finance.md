# Q1 2026 Earnings Notes

Revenue for Q1 2026 came in at 4.2 billion dollars, up 18 percent year over year. Operating margin expanded to 27 percent thanks to disciplined cost control and stronger pricing in the enterprise segment.

The CFO guided full-year revenue to a range of 18 to 19 billion dollars. Capital expenditure will rise to fund the new datacenter buildout in the Pacific Northwest, with most spending falling in the second half.

Our share of the search advertising market climbed to 11 percent, the highest in company history. The board approved a 5 billion dollar share buyback authorization expiring in December 2027.
