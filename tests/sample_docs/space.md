# Outer Space Notes

The James Webb Space Telescope launched in December 2021. It is the largest optical telescope ever placed in space and observes primarily in the infrared.

Andromeda is the nearest large galaxy to the Milky Way, about 2.5 million light-years away. It is on a collision course with our galaxy and will merge in roughly 4 billion years.

Black holes form when massive stars collapse at the end of their life cycle. The supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way is called Sagittarius A*.
